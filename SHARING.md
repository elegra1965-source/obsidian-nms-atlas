**Title:** I built ATLAS — a free, voice-driven Atlas interface with a LIVE expedition tracker (no account, works on your phone)

**Body:**

Fellow Travellers — I made a fan project I'd love your eyes on: **ATLAS**, a No Man's Sky–themed AI companion that runs in your browser.

What it does, all free, no sign-up:

- **Live Swarm expedition tracker** — real Prismatic Core progress from the Galactic Atlas API, with ranked faction standings (currently Weavers in 1st) and a countdown clock to expedition end
- **Talk to the Atlas** — tap the orb and speak; it answers out loud with lore about the Gek, Korvax, Vy'keen, Sentinels, portals, the centre of the galaxy…
- A full NMS-style HUD: procedural system scanner, rotating lore archive, official screenshot gallery, local "atmospheric conditions"
- Installs as an app on your phone (PWA)

Optional: plug in your own Anthropic API key (pennies, billed to you, never to me) and the Atlas becomes a full free-form AI you can converse with about anything.

It's completely free, no ads, no tracking, and I make nothing from it. Unofficial fan project — NMS belongs to Hello Games.
